HOW IT WORKS Three steps, then you can forget about it
01 Pick the course Search the live UCSD catalog by code or title, choose your term, and add it. Duplicate watches are ignored, so add freely.
02 We watch it every two minutes Around the clock, through the whole enrollment window. You never refresh anything, and you never sit on WebReg at 3 a.m.
03 You get an email or a text The alert names the course, the term, and how many seats opened. Verify your contact once and choose either channel — or both.
